# Backtest on JPS 7-day records

Records: 1029 stations downloaded; 735 have usable thresholds and at least 50 readings.

## How much warning does each level give before danger?

53 stations reached danger level at least once in the 7 days.

| From first… | Rises measured | Median hours to danger | 25th–75th percentile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alert | 35 | 1.5 | 0.8–2.0 |
| Warning | 34 | 0.9 | 0.5–1.5 |

## How good is the straight-line "time to danger"?

646 predictions (rising, under 12 hours out). Median error -31.9 min (negative = predicted too early); median absolute error 53.7 min; within ±30% of the true time: 43.5%.

## How many messages would a family get?

Counted over 3538 station-days (days 4–7 of each record, when every rule can run):

| Rule | Messages | Per station per week |
|---|---|---|
| Message on every level change | 1453 | 2.9 |
| + no repeat within 12 h; going down, only the all-clear after 1 h | 924 | 1.8 |
| + skip alert/warning within the usual daily high (danger always sent) | 904 | 1.8 |

With all rules, the median station sent 0 messages in those days; 580 of 735 sent none; the busiest sent 26.

11 stations showed a tide signature (two rises above alert a day, on 3+ days). The tide rule held back 27 messages; no danger message was ever held back.
